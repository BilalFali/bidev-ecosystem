# apps/main-site — editorial rules for BiDev.dev articles

Before drafting, editing, or publishing any BiDev.dev article (blog post or
troubleshooting entry), read these three files in full:

- `docs/editorial/real-content-policy.md` — mandatory rules. Never fabricate
  personal experience, projects, bugs, tests, or benchmarks. Separate
  verified fact from confirmed personal experience from reasoned analysis
  from recommendation from unverified claim. Every article needs real
  original value beyond a documentation summary.
- `docs/editorial/article-workflow.md` — the research-draft-gate-approve
  process, and the editorial quality gate to run before presenting a draft.
- `docs/editorial/writing-style.md` — the owner's confirmed voice and
  structure rules (tone, openings, how to explain code, article-type
  structure, what makes an article original). Two items at the bottom are
  still open; everything else is settled.

Never publish an article (via a one-off script or otherwise) without the
owner's explicit approval of that specific draft first.

Two already-published articles contain a fabricated personal project
("RecallAI") that does not exist anywhere else in this project:
`supabase-vs-firebase-flutter` and
`flutter-local-storage-in-2026-hive-isar-and-sharedpreferences-arent-what-you-think`.
The owner decided (2026-10-11) to leave both as published; they predate
this policy. Do not rewrite or unpublish them, and do not use either as a
style or content reference for any new article.
