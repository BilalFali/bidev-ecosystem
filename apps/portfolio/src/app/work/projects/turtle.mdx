## Overview

Turtle is an innovative bike-cab service created by Parisians for urban commuters. Book your daily journeys in the same way as traditional VTC (ride-hailing) applications, but with a greener, faster, and more affordable twist. A Turtle driver will pick you up on their bike-cab to take you to your destination, navigating through traffic efficiently while reducing your carbon footprint.

## Key Features

- **Easy Booking**: Book rides with the same simplicity as popular VTC applications
- **Real-time Tracking**: Live GPS tracking of your driver from pickup to destination
- **Eco-Friendly Transport**: Zero-emission transportation for a greener Paris
- **Cost-Effective**: More affordable than traditional taxi or VTC services
- **Faster Urban Travel**: Navigate through traffic quickly with bike-cabs
- **Route Optimization**: Smart routing for the fastest journey
- **Secure Payments**: Multiple payment methods with secure processing
- **Ride History**: Complete trip history with receipts
- **Driver Ratings**: Rate your experience and provide feedback
- **Real-time Notifications**: Stay updated on driver arrival and trip status

## Technologies Used

- **Flutter**: Cross-platform mobile development
- **Firebase**: Authentication, Cloud Messaging, and Firestore
- **Google Maps SDK**: Real-time location tracking and navigation
- **REST APIs**: Backend communication and data synchronization
- **GetX/Provider**: Efficient state management
- **Payment Gateway Integration**: Secure payment processing
- **Push Notifications**: FCM for real-time ride alerts
- **Geolocation Services**: Precise pickup and drop-off location handling

## Technical Implementation

Built with Flutter for seamless cross-platform performance on both Android and iOS, Turtle delivers a smooth user experience for booking bike-cab rides. Real-time tracking is implemented using Google Maps SDK with optimized location updates to provide accurate driver positioning without excessive battery drain.

The booking flow is streamlined for quick ride requests, with intelligent address suggestions and saved locations for frequent destinations. The architecture follows clean code principles, ensuring maintainable and scalable codebase as the service expands.

The payment system integrates securely with multiple payment providers, offering users flexibility in how they pay for their rides. Push notifications keep users informed at every step of their journey, from driver assignment to arrival.

## Impact

Turtle contributes to sustainable urban mobility in Paris by offering an eco-friendly alternative to motorized transportation. By combining the convenience of ride-hailing apps with the environmental benefits of bicycle transportation, Turtle makes green commuting accessible and practical for everyday use.

## Platform Support

Available on Google Play Store for Android devices, optimized for various screen sizes and providing reliable performance across different device configurations.
