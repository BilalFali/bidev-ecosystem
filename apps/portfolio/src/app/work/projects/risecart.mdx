## Overview

RiseCart is a comprehensive e-commerce platform that allows businesses to create their own online store for free. The platform provides a complete e-commerce solution with a mobile application for customers and management tools for business owners.

## Key Features

### Customer Mobile App

- **Free Store Creation**: Create your e-commerce website at no cost
- **Product Browsing**: Intuitive product catalog with categories
- **Shopping Cart**: Easy-to-use cart management
- **Secure Checkout**: Integrated payment processing
- **Order Tracking**: Real-time order status updates
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Simple and intuitive shopping experience

### Business Features

- **Store Management**: Complete control over your online store
- **Product Management**: Add, edit, and manage products easily
- **Order Processing**: Efficient order management system
- **Flexible Pricing**: Nominal subscription fee after free trial
- **Business Analytics**: Track sales and performance

## Technologies Used

- **Flutter**: Cross-platform mobile development
- **Stripe**: Payment processing
- **Firebase**: Backend services and authentication
- **Stripe**: Payment processing
- **Google APIs**: Location and services integration
- **REST APIs**: Server communication
