## Overview

Wegofleet Driver App is the companion application for drivers in the Wegofleet ecosystem. It enables drivers to efficiently manage ride requests, track their earnings, and provide excellent service to passengers.

## Key Features

- **Trip Management**: Accept and manage ride requests efficiently
- **Real-time Location Updates**: Continuous location tracking for accurate navigation
- **Ride Earnings Dashboard**: Detailed earnings tracking and analytics
- **Push Notifications**: Instant alerts for new ride requests
- **Background Location Tracking**: Continuous tracking even when app is in background
- **Firebase Integration**: Real-time communication and data synchronization
- **Seamless UI/UX**: Intuitive interface designed for drivers on the go

## Technologies Used

- **Flutter**: Cross-platform mobile framework
- **Firebase**: Backend services (Auth, Firestore, Messaging, Remote Config)
- **Stripe**: Payment and earnings management
- **Socket.IO**: Real-time driver-passenger communication
- **GetX**: Advanced state management
- **Google APIs**: Maps, directions, and location services
- **REST APIs**: Server communication
- **Google Maps**: Navigation and route optimization

## Technical Implementation

The driver app implements sophisticated background location tracking to ensure accurate positioning at all times. Firebase Cloud Messaging handles real-time notifications for new ride requests, while Firestore provides instant data synchronization.

The earnings dashboard gives drivers complete visibility into their income, with detailed breakdowns by trip, day, and week. The app's state management using GetX ensures smooth performance even during high-frequency updates.

## Platform Support

Available for both Android and iOS platforms, ensuring maximum driver coverage.
